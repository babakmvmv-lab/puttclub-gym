Anatomy layer (muscle view / نمایش عضله هدف)
- Adapted from the "Fit Mit With" anatomy atlas (github.com/slfresh/fitmitwith-anatomy-atlas), CC BY-SA 4.0,
  which is derived from:
  * Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0 (https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy)
  * BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan
- Changes by PuttClub: meshes fitted inside each Rocketbox character (segment mapping + radial envelope fit),
  re-skinned to the character skeletons (depth-aware weights), deep hidden muscles removed, muscle tissue subdivided,
  per-muscle fibre fields (harmonic origin->insertion + across-fibre coordinate) computed for shading.
  The adapted anatomy data (src/assets/anat/*) is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
